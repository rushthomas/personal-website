Thomas Rush Family Logistics SMS

This personal program sends enrolled adult household members a weekly Sunday-morning text summary of children's schedules, school/camp reminders, and family logistics. Occasional urgent schedule-change messages may also be sent.

How to enroll

To enroll, text YES to (817) 383-0129.

By texting YES, you agree to receive recurring automated family logistics messages from Thomas Rush Family Logistics. Message frequency is approximately one message per week, on Sunday mornings, plus occasional urgent schedule updates. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

SMS Terms and Conditions · SMS Privacy Policy

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are not shared, sold, or provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
